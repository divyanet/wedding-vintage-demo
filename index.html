Our Story

A Charleston love story

Olivia was sketching the old oaks at Magnolia Gardens when a gust scattered her charcoal pencils across the path. Henry — late for a walking tour he was leading — stopped, gathered every last one, and stayed to watch her finish the drawing.

He proposed three springs later under those same oaks, with a ring tucked inside a hollowed-out sketchbook. She drew his portrait through happy tears before she remembered to say yes.
